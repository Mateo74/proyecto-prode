Delete account / Eliminar cuenta

This page explains how to delete your Once Metros account and personal data.

English

  1. Sign in to your account.
  2. Go to My account.
  3. In Danger zone, tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm the action in the dialog.

Deletion is permanent and removes your account data and predictions.

If you cannot access your account, request deletion by email: mateomarenco74@gmail.com.

Espanol

  1. Inicia sesion en tu cuenta.
  2. Ve a Mi cuenta.
  3. En Zona de peligro, toca Eliminar mi cuenta.
  4. Confirma la accion en el dialogo.

La eliminacion es permanente y borra tu cuenta y tus predicciones.

Si no puedes acceder a tu cuenta, solicita la eliminacion por correo: mateomarenco74@gmail.com.
